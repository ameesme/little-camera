privacy

Last changed 29 October 2025

There is no account, no password and no profile. This is the whole list of what the service holds.

What we keep

  • Your email address. It is how you get back into your blog and how you are told someone asked to follow it. It is never shown on the blog and never passed on.
  • Your pictures. Exactly as the camera took them: 320 by 240, black and white, with the time they were taken.
  • The name you chose and the address your blog lives at.
  • Who reads it. The email address and the name of each person you let in, and anything they wrote under a picture.
  • What the camera last reported: its battery, how many pictures it holds, and the firmware it runs. The app shows you the same numbers.

What we do not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts on the blog, no selling anything to anyone. The emails carry no open tracking. The phone app talks to one server and nothing else.

Who can see your pictures

Only the people you have let in, through a link sent to their address. A visitor without a link sees the newest picture and nothing more. The phone holding your camera can manage the blog only after you allow it from your own inbox.

How long

Pictures stay until you delete them or delete the blog, and then they are gone from the server for good. Sign-in links expire within hours, reading links within 48 hours, and expired ones are cleared out.

Your side of it

Ask and you get a copy of everything above, or we delete it. One email is enough. Anyone reading a blog can take themselves off the list from the bottom of any message we send them.

Where it lives

On servers in the European Union. We use an email provider to deliver messages, and they see the address and the message, which is the entire point of an email provider.
