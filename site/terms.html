Little camera is a camera and a small blog that the camera posts to. These are the terms for the blog at lttl.cam. Plain language on purpose; if something here is unclear, ask before you rely on it.

Your blog

A little blog is yours. The pictures on it are yours, the comments under them belong to whoever wrote them, and nothing on it is shown to anyone you have not let in. We do not sell it, licence it, train on it or put anything of our own next to it.

Your address

A blog is held by an email address. That address is the only way back into it, so an address you lose access to is a blog you lose access to. There is no password to reset and no support queue that can prove who you are.

What you may not put on it

  • Pictures of people who have not agreed to be photographed, where that matters where you live.
  • Anything illegal where the blog is hosted or where you are.
  • Anything that harasses a person, including through the comments under your own pictures.

A blog that does any of this is removed, and so is the camera's ability to post to it.

What we promise

Very little. This is a small service run by a small number of people. It can be slow, it can be down, and a picture that never arrives is a picture that never arrives. Keep anything you cannot lose somewhere else as well — the app can save any picture to your phone.

Ending it

You can delete a blog from the app at any time, which deletes the pictures and the list of people reading it. We can end an account that breaks the rules above, and will say why.

The boring part

The service is provided as it is, without warranty. Where the law allows it, we are not liable for lost pictures, lost time or anything that follows from either. Where the law does not allow that, the law wins. Dutch law applies.
